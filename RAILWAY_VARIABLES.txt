Add these two variables in Railway -> KHOJA RENT AI service -> Variables:

ELEVENLABS_API_KEY=your_private_api_key
ELEVENLABS_VOICE_ID=your_selected_female_voice_id

Optional:
ELEVENLABS_MODEL=eleven_v3

Keep your API key private. Do not send it in Telegram or screenshots.
Existing variables such as BOT_TOKEN, ADMIN_ID, DATABASE_URL, OPENAI_API_KEY remain required.
